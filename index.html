What this does with your keystrokes

Nothing you type here is transmitted, recorded, or stored anywhere. Every key you press is timed in this browser tab's own memory — how long since the last key, how long you held this one down — and those two numbers become a pitch and a tone, right here, with the Web Audio API. This page makes no network requests after it loads. Open your browser's network panel and watch: there is nothing to see. Reload the page and every timing is gone. There is no server behind this, no analytics, and no copy of what you typed anywhere but your own screen while you're looking at it.

See README.md for exactly how the mapping works and what this does and does not claim.
